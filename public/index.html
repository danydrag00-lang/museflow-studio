HOW MUSEFLOW WORKS

Music with clarity.

What this studio can do

Museflow generates original algorithmic audio with synthesized pads, melodic patterns and optional electronic rhythms. It does not use existing copyrighted recordings, playlists or sample packs.

What “free commercial use” means

The included original synthesized instrumentals are offered for nonexclusive personal and commercial creative projects, including monetized YouTube videos. Read the music licence.

Originality safeguards

New seeds, shifting harmonic arrangements and SHA-256 exact-file checks reduce repetition against tracks produced in this studio. A new seed does not guarantee that music is different from everything ever composed; fingerprint checks catch identical files, not every similar melody.

Audio formats and availability

Install FFmpeg for MP3 encoding. Without FFmpeg, compositions export as WAV. The bundled free catalogue is composed on first playback or by the repository's catalogue-build workflow.

Private by default

This local web app runs on 127.0.0.1 unless you explicitly configure public hosting. Generated files and the track index are stored in the server's data/ folder.
